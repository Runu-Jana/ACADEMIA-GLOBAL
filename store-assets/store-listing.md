## Full description (Play, ≤ 4000 chars)

Shiksha Sarthi is India's virtual learning platform for students who want to
restart, continue or complete their education — from Class 10 to postgraduate —
with UGC-entitled online, distance and regular degrees, industry skill courses,
and structured exam preparation.

Whether you're a working professional fitting study around a job, a student
comparing colleges, or someone returning to education after a break, Shiksha
Sarthi brings the whole journey into one app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Explore UGC-entitled online & distance degree programmes from partner universities
• Compare courses, fees, durations and eligibility side by side
• Apply for admission in a simple guided flow, with EMI options at checkout
• Learn with video lessons, study material, live classes and searchable transcripts
• Track your progress automatically as you watch, and pick up where you left off
• Take tests and earn a verifiable certificate on completion
• Ask Sarthi, our AI tutor, for help that's grounded in your own course
• Discover scholarships and government-exam guidance
• Get career help: career-path finder, skill roadmaps, SOP and interview prep
• Shop books and stationery for your studies
• Study in English or हिन्दी

WHY LEARNERS CHOOSE US
• Guidance, not guesswork — transparent comparisons and honest counselling
• Free counselling and admission support (you never pay us a counselling fee)
• Certificates you can verify by serial number
• Built for India — INR pricing, EMI, Indian exams and streams

Shiksha Sarthi is an education discovery and support platform. We are not a
university; degrees are awarded by the partner institution you enrol with. Fees
are paid to and refunded by the university per its policy.

Questions? Reach our team from the in-app support section. Learn today, lead
tomorrow.
